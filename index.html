PB&J Recipe!

Learn How To Make A Simple Peanut Butter And Jelly Sandwich, In A Few Simple Steps.

Ingredients

  • 2 Slices Of White Bread
  • Peanut Butter(Smooth)
  • Jelly (Strawberry)

Instructions

  1. Lay Out Two Slices Of Bread.
  2. Spread Peanut Butter On One SLice.
  3. Spread Peanut Butter On Other SLice.
  4. Put THe Two Slices Together.
